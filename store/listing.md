**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Tilt your phone and roll the red ball to the GOAL before the clock hits zero!

Red Ball Escape turns your phone into a wooden tilt maze. There are no buttons and no joysticks: lean your phone and the ball rolls with real momentum, picks up speed on a steep tilt, coasts when you level out and bounces softly off the walls.

You have 60 seconds. Every maze you clear is worth 100 points, and the next maze is a little bigger and has more walls. How many can you escape before time runs out?

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap Start, then hold your phone flat in front of you
• Tilt it to roll the red ball through the maze
• Prefer touch? Drag your finger on the maze to steer the ball instead
• Reach the yellow GOAL square to score and move to the next maze
• Clear as many mazes as you can in 60 seconds

FEATURES
• Tilt controls with realistic rolling physics, plus touch steering
• A new random maze every level, always solvable
• Mazes grow from 5x5 up to 15x15 as you progress
• 60-second time attack with score and saved high score
• Background music and a victory chime (mute button included)
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• Small download, works offline

Perfect for a quick game while you wait, and for challenging friends to beat your high score.
```
